# Visual Exploratory Data Analysis Summary

This document summarizes the key findings from the visual exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the Olist e-commerce dataset. The objective of this phase was to understand customer purchasing behavior, operational performance, product trends, seller performance, and the relationships between key business variables before moving into feature engineering and machine learning.

---

# Customer Analysis

## Customer Spending

Customer spending exhibits a highly right-skewed distribution. Most customers spend relatively small amounts, while a small proportion of customers contribute substantially higher purchase values.

The average customer spending is **166.07**, whereas the median is **107.95**, indicating that a limited number of high-value transactions increase the overall average. Spending ranges from **9.59** to **13,664.08**, further highlighting the presence of a small premium customer segment.

From a business perspective, a significant share of revenue is generated by relatively few customers. Retaining these high-value customers through loyalty programs and personalized marketing strategies could have a meaningful impact on long-term revenue.

---

## Purchase Frequency

Purchase frequency is heavily concentrated around a single transaction. The average customer placed **1.03 orders**, while both the median and the 75th percentile remain **1 order**. Only a very small number of customers made repeat purchases, with the maximum reaching **16 orders**.

This pattern suggests that the marketplace has a large proportion of one-time buyers. Increasing customer retention and encouraging repeat purchases present an important opportunity for sustainable business growth.

---

## Average Order Value

The distribution of average order value closely resembles the spending distribution and is also strongly right-skewed. The mean order value is **161.07**, while the median is **105.78**, again reflecting the influence of a relatively small number of high-value purchases.

The **90th percentile** is **307.94**, while the **99th percentile** exceeds **1,069**, demonstrating that premium purchases represent only a small fraction of all transactions.

Since most customers place only one order, average order value is naturally very similar to total customer spending across much of the dataset.

---

## Average Review Score

Customer ratings are concentrated at the upper end of the five-point scale. The average review score is **4.10**, while both the median and mode are **5**, indicating consistently positive customer feedback.

The distribution is negatively skewed (**-1.39**), meaning high ratings occur far more frequently than low ratings. At least 75% of customers have an average review score of **5**.

Although negative reviews are relatively uncommon, they remain valuable because they often highlight issues related to product quality, logistics, or customer service.

---

## Average Delivery Time

Delivery times are positively skewed. The average delivery time is **12.51 days**, compared with a median of **10 days** and a mode of **7 days**, indicating that most orders are delivered within two weeks while a small number experience substantially longer delivery times.

The **75th percentile** is **16 days**, the **90th percentile** is **23 days**, and the **99th percentile** reaches **46 days**. The maximum recorded delivery time of **210 days** represents an exceptional case rather than typical performance.

Overall, the logistics process appears reliable for most customers, although reducing unusually long delivery times would further improve consistency.

---

## Delivery Time and Customer Satisfaction

Delivery time shows a moderate negative relationship with customer review scores. The correlation coefficient of **-0.335** indicates that longer delivery times are generally associated with lower customer ratings.

While delivery performance clearly influences customer satisfaction, it is unlikely to be the only contributing factor. Product quality, seller service, packaging, and customer expectations also play important roles.

Improving delivery speed therefore represents one practical way to enhance the overall customer experience.

---

## Customer Analysis Summary

The customer analysis reveals a marketplace characterized by low purchase frequency, consistently high customer satisfaction, and a relatively small group of high-value customers responsible for a disproportionate share of revenue. Improving customer retention and maintaining efficient delivery operations appear to offer the greatest opportunities for long-term growth.

# Order Analysis

## Order Status Distribution

### Objective

Evaluate the operational performance of the marketplace by examining the distribution of order statuses.

### Key Findings

- **97.78%** of all orders were successfully delivered.
- Only **0.47%** of orders were cancelled.
- Approximately **1.12%** remained in the shipped state at the time of data collection.
- All remaining statuses accounted for less than one percent of total orders.

### Business Insight

The exceptionally high delivery rate demonstrates strong operational efficiency and provides confidence in subsequent analyses involving customer satisfaction, delivery performance, and sales.

---

## Monthly Order Trend

### Objective

Examine changes in monthly order volume to identify long-term growth patterns and seasonal effects.

### Key Findings

- Monthly orders increased steadily throughout **2017**, reflecting rapid business growth.
- Order volume peaked in **November 2017**, most likely driven by Black Friday promotions.
- Throughout **2018**, monthly orders stabilized between approximately **6,000 and 7,200**, indicating a mature level of marketplace activity.
- The final month of the dataset was excluded because it represents an incomplete reporting period.

### Business Insight

The marketplace experienced sustained growth before reaching a stable operating level in 2018. Seasonal events such as Black Friday clearly influenced customer purchasing behaviour, highlighting the importance of inventory planning and demand forecasting.

---

## Delivery Performance

### Objective

Assess delivery performance by comparing actual delivery dates with estimated delivery dates.

### Key Findings

- **91.89%** of delivered orders arrived earlier than promised.
- **1.34%** arrived exactly on the estimated delivery date.
- Only **6.77%** were delivered after the promised date.

### Business Insight

The overwhelming majority of deliveries arrived ahead of schedule, suggesting a reliable fulfillment process and conservative delivery estimates. Since delivery performance influences customer satisfaction, maintaining this level of operational efficiency should remain a priority.

# Product Analysis

Product demand and revenue are concentrated within a relatively small number of categories. The highest-volume categories include **cama_mesa_banho**, **esporte_lazer**, **moveis_decoracao**, **beleza_saude**, and **utilidades_domesticas**.

Revenue rankings differ slightly from sales volume. Categories such as **beleza_saude** and **relogios_presentes** generate the highest revenue despite not always having the highest transaction counts. This demonstrates that popular categories are not necessarily the most profitable.

Average product prices are strongly right-skewed, with most products falling into lower price ranges and a relatively small number of premium products creating the long upper tail.

Customer ratings remain consistently high across leading categories, indicating generally strong product quality and customer satisfaction throughout the marketplace.

Overall, the marketplace performs well across both high-volume and high-value product categories. Future growth may be achieved by further strengthening categories that combine strong demand with high revenue generation.

---

# Seller Analysis

Seller performance is similarly concentrated among a relatively small number of businesses. A handful of sellers generate substantially higher revenue than the remainder of the marketplace, while many sellers contribute comparatively modest sales.

The revenue distribution follows a pronounced right-skewed pattern, demonstrating that marketplace revenue is unevenly distributed across sellers.

Despite these differences in sales performance, average seller review scores remain consistently high, suggesting that customer satisfaction is generally maintained across sellers regardless of revenue level.

Delivery performance also varies across sellers. The fastest-performing sellers achieve average delivery times of approximately one to two days, providing a useful operational benchmark for fulfillment efficiency.

Overall, the seller analysis highlights opportunities to improve the performance of smaller sellers while continuing to support the marketplace's highest-performing businesses.
